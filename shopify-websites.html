Project Overview

A design-led ecommerce project built to showcase real Shopify direction.

Green Bloom Matcha was developed as a real Shopify project to demonstrate how a premium ecommerce brand can feel editorial, soft, and conversion-aware at the same time. The store balances clean merchandising with lifestyle framing so products feel elevated without overwhelming the user journey.

Project Direction

A complete Shopify storefront designed to demonstrate ecommerce page structure, storytelling, and product merchandising in a real-world setup.

Visual Direction

Warm beige and muted green tones, restrained typography, and generous whitespace to create a soft premium feel with editorial clarity.

Intended Audience

Design-conscious lifestyle shoppers who expect a polished mobile shopping flow and product pages that feel trustworthy and calm.

Design Goal

Show how a Shopify store can combine elegant brand presence with practical ecommerce UX, clear hierarchy, and intuitive purchase pathways.

Design Approach

Design Approach

This project explores a softer direction for ecommerce.

Instead of high-contrast or aggressive layouts, the design focuses on:

  • Muted colours (matcha green + warm beige)
  • Calm visual rhythm
  • Strong product focus
  • Minimal distractions

The goal was to create a store that feels closer to a lifestyle brand than a typical ecommerce website.

Mobile experience was prioritised throughout — ensuring the layout feels natural, spacious, and easy to navigate on smaller screens.

Key Features

Clean Shopify storefront design
