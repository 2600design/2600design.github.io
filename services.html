Before we start

Web design questions, answered.

How much does a small business website cost?

At 2600 Design, a one-page Launch Website starts at A$700 and a Business Website with up to five custom pages starts at A$1,700. These are starting prices; your quote depends on the scope, content and support your project needs. Compare the website packages.

What is included in a Shopify store project?

The Shopify Store package starts at A$2,500 and includes storefront setup and styling, homepage, collection and product templates, initial setup for up to ten products, and mobile optimisation. Shopify subscription fees, domain fees, paid themes and apps are separate. Explore the Shopify design project.

Are domain names, hosting and copywriting included?

Domain and hosting fees are separate from the website design packages. Full copywriting, photography, filming and video production are also outside the listed website packages. We will agree on the content and responsibilities when defining your scope.

Will my website work on mobile and include SEO setup?

The Launch Website and Business Website packages include responsive design and basic SEO setup. The structure and layout are designed for different screen sizes, with page titles, descriptions and launch essentials considered as part of the build.

Can I work with you from Sydney or another Australian city?

Yes. I am based in Canberra and work online with businesses across Australia, including Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane. Your project brief, design feedback and launch can all be handled online, so you do not need to be nearby.

How do I start a website project?

Send a project enquiry with your business details, goals, budget and preferred timing. I work online with businesses across Australia and will help you choose between a focused launch website, a larger business website, a Shopify store or a digital product design project.
