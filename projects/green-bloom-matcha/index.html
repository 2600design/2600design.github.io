The direction

A slower mood.
A clearer storefront.

Green Bloom Matcha explores how an ecommerce brand can feel editorial and inviting while keeping products easy to find. Warm neutrals, muted greens and generous spacing establish a consistent visual direction.

The homepage introduces the brand before guiding visitors into the collection. Supporting pages carry that same rhythm through the story, product browsing and cart experience.

  • Homepage visual storytelling
  • Collection & product structure
  • Brand-led editorial layouts
  • Mobile shopping direction

The shopping journey

From the story
to the product.

The design balances lifestyle framing with clear product presentation. Content hierarchy, image placement and restrained colour help visitors move through the store without competing visual messages.

This is a design-led Shopify concept. The case study shows the storefront direction and page relationships; it does not claim measured sales or conversion results.
