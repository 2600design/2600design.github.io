My Little Shop

An accessories store with an editorial sensibility. Art direction, product discovery and Shopify theme development come together in a storefront designed to make small details worth exploring.

Project type
Independent accessories store
Scope
UX, visual design & theme development
Platform
Shopify · Customised theme
Approach
Editorial design · Product-led discovery

The brief

Character in the details.
Clarity in the journey.

My Little Shop is an independent Shopify project developed by the founder of 2600 Design. The store brings hair accessories and jewellery into a cohesive shopping experience, with the same attention given to product presentation as to the way customers find and select an item.

The brief was to create a distinct, softly editorial storefront while keeping the practical decisions of shopping clear: where to browse, which colour to choose, what a product includes and how to add it to the bag. The work spans visual direction, page layouts, product merchandising and custom Shopify theme components.

  • Storefront art direction & visual design
  • Homepage & collection layouts
  • Product pages & colourway presentation
  • Navigation & collection discovery
  • Wishlist interface & saved items
  • Reusable Shopify sections & settings

01 / Art direction

A softer setting.
A sharper product focus.

A restrained palette of blush, warm cream and off-white creates a gentle backdrop for the collection. Fine typography, generous spacing and quiet dividing lines give the storefront an editorial rhythm, allowing colour, texture and small product details to carry the visual interest.

The homepage pairs lifestyle imagery with direct routes into the range. Collection and product pages continue the same visual language, so browsing feels like moving through one considered shop. The balance is deliberate: enough atmosphere to establish a point of view, with enough structure to keep the next step easy to find.

02 / Product discovery

More than one way
to find a favourite.

The store is organised around New In, Hair, Sets, Jewellery and Shop by Fabric, with Earrings and Rings within Jewellery. This gives customers different starting points: a recent arrival, a particular accessory or the material they prefer.

Product cards bring together imagery, names, prices and colour options, helping customers compare pieces as they browse. Wishlist controls offer a way to save items and return to them later. These details support a considered shopping journey without competing with the products themselves.

03 / Product experience

Small choices,
clearly presented.

The product page places photography alongside the details needed to make a selection. Colour swatches, the selected option, quantity and add-to-cart controls sit together, giving customers a clear path from looking at a piece to choosing their version.

The theme also supports structured product information for details, composition, care and shipping, with optional content displayed when supplied. This keeps the presentation relevant to each item and gives the store owner a consistent framework for adding products.

04 / Shopify implementation

A considered storefront.
A practical foundation.

The storefront is built through Shopify theme customisation, using reusable sections and editable settings for the homepage, collection layouts and product content. This gives the store room to evolve as imagery, featured collections and individual pieces change.

Shopify remains the foundation for products, variants, inventory, cart and checkout. The custom work focuses on how that information is presented and how customers move through it, with responsive layouts that adapt the experience to smaller screens.

The project demonstrates 2600 Design’s approach to ecommerce: a visual identity carried through the full shopping journey, supported by a store structure that remains useful beyond the first collection.
