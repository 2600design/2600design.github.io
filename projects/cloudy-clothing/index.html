Overview

This project was created to develop a soft, minimal fashion ecommerce experience for a modern clothing brand. The goal was to combine clean visual presentation with a simple, intuitive shopping journey.

The work brings the store’s homepage, product pages and cart into one visual system. A custom WordPress child theme carries the brand styling, while WooCommerce provides the product and shopping structure. The focus is on consistent presentation, clear navigation and layouts that adapt across screen sizes.
