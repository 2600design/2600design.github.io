Featured Product · Web

FrameDreaming

A calm, cinematic web product for turning images and prompts into short video — designed to make the creative process feel immediate and inviting.

Web app Product UI AI creative tools

Product direction

The idea

Make ideas move.

FrameDreaming brings image-to-video and text-to-video creation into one focused web workspace. The interface keeps the magic visible while making the next action obvious.

Prompt inStart with a visual or a simple idea.
Motion outGenerate a short clip ready to share.
Clear controlsChoose model, ratio and duration.
Built for repeatSave, refine and make the next version.

Core flow

01 / INPUT

Upload or describe

Bring in a starting image or write a short prompt that captures the scene you want to see.

02 / DIRECT

Shape the result

Pick the model, aspect ratio and duration without burying the creative decision under settings.

03 / GENERATE

Download and iterate

Watch the generation progress, review the result and keep moving through the next idea.

Interface principles

01 · Focus

One clear next step

The workspace guides people from idea to output without making the product feel technical or intimidating.

02 · Confidence

Show the cost up front

Model, quality and duration stay understandable, so people know what they are choosing before they generate.

03 · Atmosphere

Calm, cinematic energy

Deep ocean tones, soft light and generous spacing give the product a creative mood without distracting from the work.

What I worked on

  • Product positioning and visual direction
  • Information architecture and page structure
  • Prompt-to-generation user flow
  • Generation settings and credit clarity
  • Responsive web interface
  • Product landing and pricing experience

A product with room to grow

FrameDreaming is designed as a focused web experience: simple enough to start in seconds, considered enough to support a creative habit. The system can continue to grow around the same core promise — less friction between an idea and the moment it starts moving.
