The idea

Make the next
creative step clear.

FrameDreaming is a creative product I designed and built for turning text, images and guided templates into video and imagery. The challenge is to make technical choices understandable without taking attention away from the idea.

The flow connects the starting point, generation settings, credit information and recent creations. Model, format, duration and other options stay visible before a generation begins.

  • Product positioning & interface design
  • Text and image generation flows
  • Generation settings & credit clarity
  • Responsive web and iOS experiences

The product

One workspace.
Room to explore.

The web experience gives the prompt and generation controls a clear hierarchy, with a place to preview the result. On iOS, the same decisions are arranged for a smaller screen, with templates and recent creations close at hand.

FrameDreaming is still in development. The interface studies and clips below show the current product direction and examples made with it; a public product link will follow when it is ready.
