PTE Score

A clearer path from practice to progress. A bilingual learning product that brings exam-style tasks, audio practice and useful feedback into one connected experience.

The challenge

Four skills.
One clear next step.

Speaking needs recording controls. Writing needs space to think and a visible timer. Listening needs audio that is easy to follow. A learning product has to support these different tasks without making the interface feel unfamiliar each time.

I organised PTE Score around a repeatable loop: choose a task, practise, read the feedback and return to the areas that need attention. A shared structure connects the experience across web and iOS.

  • Product structure & navigation
  • Exam-style practice interfaces
  • Audio and recording workflows
  • Bilingual content & progress

The experience

Feedback that
leads somewhere.

The home screen gives learners a starting point, while skill categories and question types make the practice library easier to navigate. Recording, listening and writing screens each get controls suited to the task.

AI-assisted feedback and practice history sit within the same journey, so a result can inform the next attempt. The product brings content, feedback and repetition together rather than treating them as separate destinations.
