The direction

An editorial eye.
An everyday wardrobe.

Somer House is a self-initiated studio project exploring how a fashion storefront can carry the feeling of a print editorial. The aim was to give the imagery space, establish a distinct mood and make the next step feel natural.

Warm ivory, charcoal and muted terracotta sit alongside large serif headlines and small, spaced navigation labels. A centred wordmark, quiet rules and generous margins bring the same visual rhythm to campaign stories and product browsing.

The shopping experience

From a feeling
to a favourite.

The homepage leads with a seasonal campaign, then moves into editorial notes and selected pieces. Collection pages keep the same calm tone, with portrait imagery, clear product information and filtering that stays close to the products.

The theme extends that language through product details, search, journal pages and the cart. Responsive layouts adapt the hierarchy for smaller screens, while quick view offers a shorter route from browsing to a closer look.

  • Campaign & editorial layouts
  • Collection filtering & sorting
  • Product details & quick view
  • Responsive storefront design

Built to be edited

A visual system.
A flexible theme.

Built with Liquid sections and JSON templates for Shopify Online Store 2.0, the theme includes editable homepage modules and settings for colour and content width. Native Shopify objects and forms underpin the product, collection and cart templates.

This project brings visual direction and theme development into one study: a consistent storefront that can accommodate a new campaign, a different collection or the next chapter of a brand.

Self-initiated concept using AI-generated campaign imagery and demonstration product data. Developed through reference studies of DÔEN and other editorial storefronts; no brand affiliation is implied. Validated in a local theme preview; live-store launch and merchant testing are outside this study.
